What happens on the discovery call?

We’ll chat about your symptoms, history and goals. I’ll share what I think could be going on and whether working together makes sense. There’s no pressure to sign up.

Do I need recent blood tests?

Recent results are a great starting point. If you don’t have any, or they’re missing key markers, I’ll guide you on what to ask for. Confirm how clients get their bloods done

What are ‘functional ranges’?

Standard lab ranges are built to flag disease, so they’re wide. Functional ranges are narrower and help spot early imbalances, which is why you can feel unwell even when your results come back ‘normal’.

Are sessions online?

Yes. Consults and check-ins happen online, so you can work with me from home, wherever you are. Confirm online/in-person options

How soon will I feel a difference?

Everyone is different. Many women notice changes in energy and digestion within the first few weeks, while deeper hormone work takes longer. We track your progress every week.

Can I work with you alongside my GP?

Absolutely. This work complements your medical care. It doesn’t replace it. Keep your GP in the loop, and never stop prescribed medication without their advice.
